**Balatro High Stakes** (formerly Balatro Multiplayer 3P) is an unofficial fork of Balatro Multiplayer by Trust Ben Dover. Credits to Virtualized and the Balatro Multiplayer team (GPL-3.0). It is not affiliated with LocalThunk, Playstack or the Balatro Multiplayer team.

## What's new in 0.6.7
- **2 to 8 players.** Lobbies (including Steam lobbies) now hold up to 8 players. The lobby list and in-game HUD are reworked for up to 7 opponents. With 6 or more players alive, the bottom two lose a life each PvP blind.
- **1v1 Duel.** A clean duel for exactly 2 players, like classic Attrition.
- **Teams.** Play 2v2, 3v3, 4v4, 2v2v2 or 2v2v2v2. Teams share lives, the team's total score counts, and Nemesis effects never hit your teammates. The host can shuffle the teams and pick the team size.
- **Deathmatch.** Everyone plays every PvP blind against everyone else, down to the last one standing. Leaving eliminates you. If you disconnect, you have 60 seconds to rejoin before you're out. There's no late join. Eliminated players can watch the live standings or go back to the lobby while the match goes on.
- **King of the Hill.** Win PvP blinds to take the crown, then hold it to win. It includes a bounty and a crown shield, and the hold target adjusts to the number of players.
- **7 new multiplayer jokers:** Third Wheel, Vulture, Laughing Third, Kingslayer, Bounty Hunter, Middle of the Pack and Triumvirate. They have original pixel art and scale with the number of rivals.
- **Renamed to Balatro High Stakes.** Your settings, install folder and `.env` stay the same.
- **Bug fixes:**
  - An eliminated player pressing "Return to Lobby" no longer ends the match for everyone.
  - If all remaining players die in the same blind, the highest score now wins (before, nobody won).
  - Starting a new game while a match is running is now blocked.

## Important
- **Everyone (host and friends) must be on 0.6.7.** 0.6.6 and 0.6.7 cannot play together.
- **Already on 0.6.6?** Click the **Update available** button in the main menu, or run `Update.bat`. Your `.env` settings are kept.
- **New install:** download the Installer zip, unzip it and run `Install.bat`.
- **Known limitation:** if the **host** leaves or closes Balatro, a Steam match still ends for everyone. Host migration is planned for 0.6.8.
