Balatro High Stakes (formerly "Balatro Multiplayer 3P") is a modified version of:

- **Balatro Multiplayer** (the mod, in `mod/`) — <https://github.com/Balatro-Multiplayer/BalatroMultiplayer>,

- **BalatroMultiplayerAPI-Server** (in `server/`) — <https://github.com/Balatro-Multiplayer/BalatroMultiplayerAPI-Server>,
  by the Balatro Multiplayer team, GPL-3.0.

Modified from October 2026 by **Trust Ben Dover**: 3-player Attrition lobbies, rejoin / late join,
a Lua port of the server that runs inside the host's game, Steam peer-to-peer networking
(lobbies, invites, ISteamNetworkingMessages via LuaJIT FFI), Game Over fixes for 3 players,
an auto-updater and an installer; from 0.6.7: 2 to 8 players, 1v1 Duel, Teams, King of the Hill and
special multiplayer jokers. Per-file details: [mod/FORK_NOTICE.md](mod/FORK_NOTICE.md),
[server/FORK_NOTICE.md](server/FORK_NOTICE.md), [mod/CHANGELOG.md](mod/CHANGELOG.md).
